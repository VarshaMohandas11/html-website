When did taco first appear in the united stated?

The origins of the taco are debated, but most historians trace the modern dish to 18th-century Mexican silver mines, where the term "taco" originally referred to paper-wrapped gunpowder charges used in mining.The taco's journey to the United States began in 1905(source: Google crome) with Mexican migrants working in mines and railroads, where it was popularized as street food by vendors known as "Chili Queens" in San Antonio and Los Angeles.
